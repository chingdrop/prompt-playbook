Timeframe relevance:
Likelihood: (Low/Med/High) + rationale
Impact: (Low/Med/High) + rationale
RAG rating: (Red/Amber/Green) + rationale
Current controls / mitigations observed:
Recommended mitigations (if requested):
Owner (role, not person): [ROLE]
Due date / review date:
Status: (Open / Monitoring / Closed)

## Rules
- Separate verified facts from allegations; label allegations explicitly.
- Use role titles, not private individual names.
- Keep statements decision-relevant and proportionate.
