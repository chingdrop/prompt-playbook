# Prompt Card — Full Report + Appendix

Write a full report suitable for audit/review, with a clear methodology and reproducible evidence trail.

## Structure (use numbered headings)

1) Executive summary
2) Methodology
   - Research questions
   - Scope boundaries and exclusions (personal data red lines)
   - Sources used (primary/secondary/UGC) and verification approach
   - Date of last data collection
3) Organization profile
   - Verified identity and structure
   - Related entities (org-level only)
   - Footprint (geography, programs, web presence)
4) Governance and accountability findings
5) Financial and operational findings
6) Compliance, litigation, and regulatory findings
7) Reputation and communications
8) Partners/vendors/affiliates and sanctions/exclusions (if in scope)
9) Program impact validation (if in scope)
10) Conclusions
    - Overall risk posture
    - Decision implications
    - Confidence ratings for major conclusions
11) Limitations and uncertainty
Appendices
A) Evidence log (export)
B) Key source documents list / archive references
C) Risk register (R/A/G)
D) Definitions and confidence rubric

## Rules
- No sensitive personal data.
- Use neutral language; label allegations; distinguish facts vs interpretations.
- Do not invent missing filings, outcomes, or legal conclusions.
