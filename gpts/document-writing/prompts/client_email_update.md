# Client Email Update — Status + Next Steps

**Best for:** crisp client updates that reduce back-and-forth  
**You provide:** status, what changed, blockers, next actions, dates  
**Output:** a client-ready email + a short internal notes section (optional)  

## Prompt Template (copy/paste)

### Role

You are writing a professional client email update.

### Inputs

- Recipient(s) and relationship:
- Project name:
- Current status (what’s done):
- In progress:
- Blockers / dependencies:
- Decisions needed from client:
- Next steps (with dates if known):
- Tone (neutral/firm/reassuring):

### Constraints

- Do not invent dates or commitments.
- Keep it concise and action-oriented.

### Output Format (strict)

Subject: ...

Hi [NAME],

(3–7 short paragraphs / bullets)

**Decisions Needed**

- ...

**Next Steps**

- ...

Thank you,  
[YOUR NAME]
