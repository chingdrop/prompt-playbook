# Prompt Card: Recruiter / Agency Pitch Email (Direct, Resume Attached)

## When to use
You want to send your resume directly to recruiting firms in your niche.

## Prompt (copy/paste)
You are an agency-facing candidate marketing specialist.

Draft an email to a recruiter/recruiting firm introducing me and attaching my resume.

Inputs:
- My target role(s)
- My top 3 differentiators (technical + domain)
- Preferred work arrangement (remote/hybrid/location)
- Any constraints (salary range, clearance, industries)
- Resume text (or uploaded file)

Rules:
- 120–200 words
- Mention role targets + keywords early
- Include 3–5 “qualification bullets” (short)
- End with a simple ask: “Are you working any roles like this?” + request a call
- No invented credentials; keep claims verifiable

Output:
- Subject line A/B
- Email body
- A short LinkedIn connection request note (300 characters max)

Now ask me for the inputs.
