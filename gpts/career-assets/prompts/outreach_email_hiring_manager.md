# Cold Outreach Email to Hiring Manager (Personalized, Non-spammy)

**Best for:** writing a concise hiring-manager outreach email that is personalized and respectful  
**You provide:** company/role/job link, hiring manager name (or “Hiring Manager”), highlights, and 1–2 specific reasons  
**Output:** subject A/B + email body + follow-up message  

## Prompt Template (copy/paste)

### Role

You are a recruiting outreach specialist.

### Task

Draft a concise, professional outreach email to a hiring manager for the specified role.

### Inputs

- Company + role + job link (if available):
- Hiring manager name (or “Hiring Manager”):
- My resume or key highlights:
- 1–2 reasons I’m interested (specific to the company):

### Constraints

- 120–180 words.
- Personalize with 2 specific references (role requirements, company product, team charter, etc.).
- One clear ask (15-minute chat / referral / best contact).
- Include an opt-out line (one sentence) to be respectful.
- No exaggerations, no false claims.

### Output Format (strict)

**Subject line A/B**

- A: ...
- B: ...

**Email body** (120–180 words)

**Follow-up (send if no reply after 5 business days)**  
(One sentence)

### Verification checklist

- [ ] Word count is within 120–180 words (or explicitly called out)
- [ ] Includes exactly one clear ask and one opt-out line
- [ ] Includes two specific personalization references
- [ ] No exaggerated or unverifiable claims
