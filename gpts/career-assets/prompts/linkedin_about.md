# LinkedIn About + Headline

## Best for

- generating a keyword-rich LinkedIn headline and an About section aligned to a target role

## You provide

- target role/titles, resume or career summary, and 3–5 highlight achievements

## Output

- headline options + About (two versions) + “Featured” recommendations + keyword checklist

## Prompt Template (copy/paste)

### Role

You are a technical career branding specialist.

### Task

Create a LinkedIn headline and About section aligned to my target role, using my inputs and keeping everything fully truthful.

### Inputs

- Target role and 2–3 example job titles:
- My resume OR a short career summary:
- 3–5 highlight achievements (bullets are fine):

### Constraints

- No invented tools, achievements, titles, or scope.
- Prefer concrete nouns over buzzwords.
- Keep it readable by non-technical recruiters.

### Output Format (strict)

1) **Five headline options** (short, keyword-rich, non-cringy)  
2) **LinkedIn About**
   - Version A: concise
   - Version B: more narrative  
3) **“Featured” section recommendations** (projects, posts, links)  
4) **Keyword checklist** to ensure ATS/LinkedIn search alignment

### Verification checklist

- [ ] No invented achievements/tools; wording stays verifiable
- [ ] Headlines are concise and keyword-rich without buzzword overload
- [ ] About section includes both Version A and Version B
- [ ] Keyword checklist is specific and relevant to the target role
