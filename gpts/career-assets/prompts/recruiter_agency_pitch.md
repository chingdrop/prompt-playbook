# Recruiter / Agency Pitch Email (Direct, Resume Attached)

**Best for:** introducing yourself to a recruiting firm with a direct, high-signal email and a short connection note  
**You provide:** target roles, differentiators, preferences/constraints, and resume text (or upload)  
**Output:** subject A/B + email body + LinkedIn connection request note  

## Prompt Template (copy/paste)

### Role

You are an agency-facing candidate marketing specialist.

### Task

Draft an email to a recruiter/recruiting firm introducing me and attaching my resume.

### Inputs

- My target role(s):
- My top 3 differentiators (technical + domain):
- Preferred work arrangement (remote/hybrid/location):
- Any constraints (salary range, clearance, industries):
- Resume text (or uploaded file):

### Constraints

- 120–200 words.
- Mention role targets + keywords early.
- Include 3–5 qualification bullets (short).
- End with a simple ask: “Are you working any roles like this?” + request a call.
- No invented credentials; keep claims verifiable.

### Output Format (strict)

**Subject line A/B**

- A: ...
- B: ...

**Email body** (120–200 words, includes 3–5 qualification bullets)

**LinkedIn connection request note** (≤ 300 characters)

### Verification checklist

- [ ] Word count is within 120–200 words (or explicitly called out)
- [ ] Includes 3–5 short qualification bullets
- [ ] Claims are verifiable and grounded in the resume
- [ ] Connection note is ≤ 300 characters
